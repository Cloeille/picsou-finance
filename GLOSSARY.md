# Picsou

A self-hosted personal-finance dashboard for an individual or a small family: it gathers
accounts from banks, brokers and crypto into one net worth.

## Language

**Integration**:
A provider Picsou can sync from, switched on or off as one unit by the admin. Market data
is not an Integration.
_Avoid_: Connector, provider (when you mean the admin switch)

**Enabled**:
The state of an Integration the admin lets Picsou sync on its own: scheduled, at startup or
from the assistant. Pressing Sync still works when it is Disabled.
_Avoid_: Configured, active, connected
