# Picsou

Personal-finance domain language: the accounts a household holds, how banks report them, and how they add up to net worth.

## Language

### Cards

**Card**:
An account that records the purchases made with one payment card, whatever way the bank debits them. A card is a liability when it carries an amount still owed, never an asset.
_Avoid_: Card compartment, credit card (for a debit card), immediat_debit / delayed_debit (bank-internal labels)

**Card nature**:
How a card's purchases are debited from the current account: immediately, in one deferred monthly debit, or through a revolving credit line.
_Avoid_: Debit mode

**Settlement**:
The single monthly debit on the current account that pays off a deferred-debit card's purchases for the period.
_Avoid_: Card total, prélèvement carte (in prose)

**Current account**:
The account a card is attached to and is debited from.
_Avoid_: CAV (in prose), checking account (in user-facing French copy)
